The lane, plainly

This room answers questions about its admitted documents — policies, guidelines, labels, manuals, studies — each speaking in its own institutional voice, never blended, with exact passages. It does not advise on patients: questions about a particular patient are refused at the door, and a document carrying a patient identifier is refused before it is stored or sent anywhere — while ordinary clinical language in a guideline or policy is admitted, and a document that merely resembles a case note is held for inspection so you can look and rule. Whether two sources disagree is a judgment: the model may propose it, you rule on it — the page never claims it on its own.
