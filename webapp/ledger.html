What this is, exactly

Your record of observed activity: pages displayed, documents and results opened, selections, edits and confirmed saves. These events do not establish reading, comprehension or preference. Every entry is timestamped twice: the page’s clock and the store’s receive clock stay separate, appended to a file in your store and sealed into the encrypted Vault with your writing.

What it never records: individual keystrokes or their values, cursor positions, selected text, document bodies or events outside this application. Obvious credential patterns in metadata are omitted with a named gap before browser storage; pattern matching cannot detect every secret. The switch is in Settings. The event kinds are session start/end, page shown, place opened/closed, document opened, result viewed, tab hidden/visible, import attempted, question followed, material carried, edit applied, search performed, capture gap, activity state/interval, material selected, document edited and document saved. Pending metadata stays in this browser until explicit server acknowledgement; repeated delivery is deduplicated. The browser outbox is outside the encrypted Vault until delivered. Off stops new capture and transmission while retaining previously captured pending records, including memory-only rows when browser storage is unavailable.

Foreground intervals are coarse per-page estimates under visibility_focus_idle.v1. A visible, focused page becomes idle after 60 seconds without an input, pointer-down or scroll occurrence; no individual gesture or its content is stored. Estimates checkpoint every 15 seconds and close on focus, visibility or page-exit boundaries. More than 30 seconds between checks, or a discontinuous clock change, leaves an explicit unobserved gap. A crash can lose the tail after the last checkpoint; no ending or duration is invented on reopen. Intervals carry the most recently displayed page/document/result context; that association does not prove it was read. Separate pages may overlap and are not a unique measure of your attention. Frames or browser capabilities that do not report activity can leave incomplete estimates.

Two sibling stores keep what the Ledger deliberately will not, under the same switch and the same seal: the words you type into the search boxes are kept whole in the capture store, and every saved revision of a document is retained by the notebook — your earlier wording is never labelled “rejected”; an edit is candidate feedback, original and revision kept side by side and judged only under your review. Off stops both the moment it stops the Ledger, and erases nothing.
