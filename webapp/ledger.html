What this is, exactly

The behavioral layer the big platforms harvest in the dark, captured here in the light and owned by you: which doors you opened, which pages you stood in, which documents and results had your attention, and when it came and went. Every entry is timestamped twice — the page’s clock and the store’s receive clock — appended to a file in your store that is never rewritten, and sealed into the encrypted Vault with your writing.

What it never records: keystrokes, the content of anything, or a single event from outside this application. The switch is in the workspace’s Settings; off means nothing new is recorded and everything already written is kept. The exact event kinds: session start and end, page shown, place opened and closed, document opened, result viewed, attention leaving and returning to the tab — and, since the capture ruling: import attempted, question followed, material carried, edit applied, search performed.

Two sibling stores keep what the Ledger deliberately will not, under the same switch and the same seal: the words you type into the search boxes are kept whole in the capture store, and every saved revision of a document is retained by the notebook — your earlier wording is never labelled “rejected”; an edit is candidate feedback, original and revision kept side by side and judged only under your review. Off stops both the moment it stops the Ledger, and erases nothing.
