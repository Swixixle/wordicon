What this is
Open Case and EthicalAlt stay their own systems, outside this record's private membrane. Here you register each one as a named instrument with a key you pin yourself, pull one signed record at a time by your own press, and keep what arrived byte for byte. Their records sit in separate seats — an Open Case signal stays Open Case's, an EthicalAlt concern level stays EthicalAlt's — and nothing is joined across them until you declare that two named things are one. After a declaration, and only then, the room computes a mechanical timeline that names its source records. Nothing on this page calls a model, polls, or refreshes.
