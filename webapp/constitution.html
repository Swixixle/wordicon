Bring Nikodemus a feeling with no name and it forges you concept readings worth arguing with — the idea's anatomy under a plain working title. Bring it a book and the book stays a book — byte-intact, mechanically read, every sentence an anchor you can hold a claim against. Bring it a recording and the transcript scrolls under the sound, one click from any sentence to its exact second. Coin a word at the Bench when you want one, cross a passage into evidence, walk the Map of everywhere your thinking has been, enter a work the way you'd enter a room — and every step leaves a record you can reopen, dispute, and re-rule, because in here the record is the product. Nikodemus is a private, evidence-bearing workshop: it began as a word-coiner, and use revealed that naming was only one small part of the work — so it is now concept-first and coinage-optional. An idea is allowed to exist without a coined name; a name is a handle for a concept, not the concept itself, attached at the Bench when wanted; and "the phrase you already have is sufficient" counts as success. What accumulates is the concepts, the sources, the documents, the recordings, and the record of what you decided and why.

Three laws hold everywhere in it: the critic advises and never decides; anything the tool isn't sure of says so instead of quietly rounding up; and nothing changes without a visible choice — no ruling recorded, no meaning rewritten, no check re-run and no page reset except by a thing you clicked. Your ruling settles things, and only your ruling, and you are allowed to change it — changes of mind are kept, never overwritten. Nothing here flatters you and nothing here decides for you; what it offers is rarer — a place where your thinking is taken seriously enough to be kept.

The record keeps its clocks. Every ruling made since September 2, 2026 carries the time it was made and the epoch the owner declared the record to be in; a deep run keeps its own receipt, Friction's objection to the input included; every input says how it arrived — typed, attached, or through a door. Older rows carry none of this and are never rewritten: their missing clocks are a finding, not a gap to paper over. Acceptances the identity migration found receipt-only wait in the Recovery Review, where a definition comes from the owner or not at all.

The record measures itself before it is used. Every definition is an event — the exact words, the concept, the time, the origin, what it supersedes — and the shelf is what those events rebuild, checked against them; older entries carry one labeled baseline, never an invented history. Every receipt names the prompt templates behind it by stage and hash, never the prompt itself. Now every new relation on the Map says who put it there — the pipeline, the owner, a model's proposal — and cites the receipt or ruling that produced it; older relations are labeled unknown and left alone. A deep run keeps a receipt like any other run. Encounter recording is off until you turn it on in this panel; while off nothing is written, and the flip itself is recorded. A recovery case ruled “not enough survives” stays findable and can be reopened by a later ruling that cites it.

Nikodemus may hear what you deliberately say to it. Press the microphone and it listens; press again and it stops; what it heard appears in the box for you to correct before it goes anywhere; the recording stays in this page's memory and is kept only if you choose to keep it. Capture is not interpretation, and interpretation is not memory until you keep it. The engine runs on this machine and names itself and its model on every transcript; the vocabulary it is primed with is your own — the name, the words you declared, what you have open, the titles you pinned, then the shelf as space remains — and every transcript cites the exact list it was told and where each word came from, because that list biases what is heard. What the engine heard stays visible beside your correction; your correction is what is sent; the record never claims the engine heard the correction. No conversation mode, no spoken replies, no camera — each of those is its own ruling.

Words go where you send them. Once something is in the box, a row of destinations appears — Research outside Nikodemus, Search my record, Develop the idea, Start a Room, Write from this, Save as an open question, and for a name with a date, Study the name and the rest. The record reads the words' shape and highlights one; it never chooses. Nothing runs until you choose, and a destination that is not built yet says so instead of doing something else. Reading the shape asks no model and writes nothing — the draft lives in this browser until you send it. Search my record is exact text in your own record, never the web; research beyond the record is a separate door, not built yet. How the words arrived — typed, attached, later spoken — travels beside them and changes nothing about where they may go.

Nikodemus can hold what your other instruments produce. Open Case and EthicalAlt are connected as instruments, not absorbed: each keeps its database, its code, its interface, its vocabulary and its signing key. What crosses is a deposition — the exact bytes a producer signed, pulled one object at a time when you press, verified under a public key you pinned here out of band, kept whole with its content hash. A verified deposition is testimony by a named witness, never a Nikodemus finding: an Open Case label stays Open Case's, an EthicalAlt concern level stays EthicalAlt's, an allegation stays paired with its response, a documented gap stays a gap, and an instrument that cannot be reached is a failure with its class — never “nothing found”. Names are not identities: a match between two instruments' records is proposed by exact name and declared, rejected or left unresolved by you alone; a timeline across both appears only after your declaration. Nothing is polled, nothing refreshes itself, and reading here never makes an instrument act. Paste a case or profile address into the box and the chooser offers the import; the Investigation Room is at /investigation.

The first minute: type into the box below — a feeling you don't have a word for, a word you're curious about, a handful of loose words, or a whole passage — choose Develop the idea, and press Run it. Cards come back; open them, argue with them, rule on them or don't. Or skip all of that, press ⤢ full page, and just write — everything else will still be here.

The whole tool is one loop, and every part below is a station on it: encounter something (Documents), examine it (runs and the Bench), keep what survives (the Library), connect it (the Map — and, at the scale of a whole book rather than a word, the Work Room), and use it in your own writing (the room) — which is where the next encounter starts.

Bringing things in Every intake is a deliberate act: nothing is read, heard, or examined until you hand it over, and what arrives carries how it arrived.
Bringing something — where examination starts

Run it — type anything. The tool reads the shape of what you gave it: a described feeling gets forged into names, loose words get collided, a long passage gets split into its separate ideas. It tells you how it read you, and one tap corrects it.

Type a word that already exists — television, parole — and it takes the word apart instead: roots, first appearance, how the sense moved, the forms built off it, its relatives, its register. This is the one stage whose claims have a right answer, so anything carrying a date or a named coiner comes back unverified unless the reviewer stakes it and says where one search settles it. A word that is not established gets told so, not given an invented past.

Go deep — the full workup: components with existing neighbors named, a critique of your input exactly as you gave it, and candidates for every component. Slower, for the ideas that deserve it.

Documents — where things enter

Documents takes in whole works — a book, a chapter, a saved news article — and keeps them as themselves. Bringing one in costs zero model calls: the file is stored byte-intact forever, split deterministically into sections, paragraphs and sentences, and indexed. The same file processed under the same extractor and segmenter revisions yields the same anchors; a changed revision creates a new immutable reading and never rewrites the old one. Whatever the reader skipped or couldn't parse is written on the document's face. Search here is your library only, exact text — not meanings, and never the web.

Select a passage and it can cross into a note, claim, citation, or Bench ingredient — an immutable reference to that exact span, retrieved fresh every time it renders; if the stored record and the document ever disagree, the mismatch is shown, not smoothed. A claim made while reading records only that it was made while viewing the span — presence is not support. Support stays unruled until you summon the one narrow question: what bearing does this exact span have on this exact claim? The answer runs on two axes — bearing (supports, contradicts, contextualizes, unrelated, or the span is insufficient: the honest negatives a support-only vocabulary once made impossible) and, when the bearing is operative, mode: direct, inference, interpretation, or speculation — unrelated and insufficient-span rulings carry no invented mode. The stage must name the sentences its judgment rests on; if they lie outside your selection it is forced to “insufficient span” in code, with the wider selection suggested and never attached invisibly. It cannot assign confidence, truth, or a score; anything extra it attaches is stripped in code with a finding. And you are never its clerk: adopt a proposal, reject it (the claim stays unruled, the rejection is kept), or rule it yourself with no model involved — every ruling carries its origin, and revising later appends a superseding ruling, earlier ones kept. Retracting any crossing is append-only and recoverable. Beyond that, what a document means is a later stage; this one only promises that what you read is what arrived.

Media — where listening enters

Media keeps recordings you own — a lecture, an interview, an episode you have the file for — byte-intact, zero model calls, exactly like Documents. Beside each recording sit its transcripts, and the constitution is blunt: the transcript is a derivative that can be wrong; the recording remains the source. Every version — publisher-supplied, platform captions, locally generated, your own correction — is a separate immutable derivation whose origin you declare; a correction is a new version beside the old one, never over it. Click any sentence and the player lands on that second; while it plays, the sentence under the sound stays lit. Select sentences and the moment becomes a time-anchored crossing — note, claim, citation, or Bench ingredient — pinned to a segment range of one named transcript version at its exact seconds, retrieved fresh every time it renders, drift shown rather than smoothed. A crossing's play button plays only its span, then stops: it testifies to those seconds, not the ones after. Claims are born unruled and take your ruling on the same two axes as everywhere else. Two layouts, one player: Media fills half gives the recording the whole research half; Media + research puts the transcript and crossings beneath it — switching restyles the same element, so playback never stops, and the split workspace carries it beside your writing with no third pane.

Where the work happens Five scales of one activity — a run’s result, a draft, a whole work, a room of separate authorities, a single kept word. You may enter at any of them.
What comes back

Anchor fit is one narrow question, asked plainly. It compares a claim against the one quoted span the component chose — not against your whole text, and not against the world. It has five answers and they are drawn as five different things: the anchor supports the claim; it supports only part of it; it concerns the subject but does not support the claim; it conflicts with the claim; or fit was not checked. Where the anchor does not fully carry a claim, the card says what is missing, in the reviewer's own recorded words where it has them — labelled as words a reviewer selected, never as a proof. It is advisory and it never blocks a ruling.

Three layers, always. Bone — checkable facts from admitted sources; often empty, and empty is honest. Flesh — the meaning: definition, tension, axiom; interpretation labelled as interpretation. Friction — a critic that attacks every candidate, tags it kitchen word or seminar term, and hides nothing. Each card opens with three rows pointing the same way — grounded, well-made, unclaimed — and a plain-terms box: the idea in one ordinary breath, plus an invented example — a sentence the model wrote to show the word in use, labelled as invented in the text itself, because it used to arrive in quotation marks and read like something you had written. Where a candidate is well made and nothing established it, the craft row says so in the same breath: craft coherent; source warrant absent.

And where a review searched, the panel says only what it can actually observe. That a result came back and that the prose cited it are two different facts and are recorded separately. What the provider fetched, and what the model read, are opaque — the search happens inside the provider — so they are printed as not applicable and unknown, never as a zero, because a zero is a measurement and no measurement was taken. The reviewer's own account of its work is labelled a model self-report; where it disagrees with the record, the record wins.

And a run opens on the work, not on the machinery. How a component was pulled out of your text — the anchor, the constraint, the context recalled — folds behind one line that says what it holds. A passing check may be quiet; a failing check may not. An anchor that was not found in your text, a constraint the anchor cannot carry, a passage that denies its own component: those stay on the page, and they are in the record too, so reopening the run a month later shows the same warnings it showed while it ran.

And a page that explains itself on every card teaches the same lesson a hundred times. So a fact stays on the face and the explanation of the fact folds behind a small mark you press once — never a tooltip, which needs a mouse and says nothing to a screen reader. A finding never goes behind it.

Each card also names the one door that could change what it says, and the others fold. Where nothing the app can do repairs a finding — a quote that is not in your text was chosen when the passage was taken apart, and no button here re-runs that check — it says so, rather than offering five actions that travel away from the problem while looking like they address it.

The four buttons on a card

Make it a word — compresses a multi-word term into one speakable coin; the plain gloss is the contract the coin must carry.

⤷ Sprout — the rabbithole: the same thread in other myths, books, poems, history. Each thread arrives split three ways — what the source shows, the reading laid over it, and what's missing — so a description of a myth can't borrow the credibility of the quote beside it. Quotes are labelled recall-unverified or paraphrase with a place to check. Threads are written from recall; the review of them is handed a live web-search tool, and whatever it consulted is listed under the result — an empty list means it consulted nothing, and the page says so in those words. Rabbitholes keep their trail, and every thread and door can sprout again.

⇄ Refract — the same idea through other languages, Spanish on every run. For each language the nearest real term in its own script, what it keeps, what it loses, and whether some language already has one word for your whole concept. A term the reviewer won't stake as attested cannot hold, and a real term that settles what your concept leaves open cannot hold either — attestation is not fit. A required language that fails to appear is reported as the stage failing to comply, which is different from that language having nothing. Terms start as recall; the reviewer is handed a live web-search tool and whatever it consulted is listed under the result — an empty list means the review is recall, which the page states outright.

◈ Archetype — the figure the concept implies. Every facet says what it rests on — a source, a documented tradition, or invention — and a tradition claim whose reference is too vague to look up is demoted to invention in code. It must name what it excludes and supply a falsifier; without one it is reported unfalsifiable, because a figure nothing can contradict describes no one. Beneath it, things that fit — characters and works, each with where it breaks, the unlocatable ones marked remembered rather than cited.

✓ Verify — turns the critic on itself, checking Friction's own claims against live sources, as many times as you click. Nothing runs it for you.

What is checked in code rather than asked of the model

A model asked to follow a rule complies most of the time, which is the same as not following it. These run after the model answers, so no wording gets past them.

Presence is not support. Two separate checks run on any quote pulled from your text: a mechanical one — is this string actually in what you gave me — and a narrow model one — does that quote support the claim resting on it. They render as two visibly different things, and a green first check never stands in for the second.

Craft and source-drift stay on separate axes. Where a definition claims more than the quoted span shows, that lands under Beyond the source and never feeds the verdict. Each concept records the text's own stance, so a candidate indicting what its source blesses arrives labelled a counter-reading rather than passing as extraction.

A sprout thread claiming to hold while your concept's own recorded parts are absent from the source is demoted to strained — in code, not left to the reviewer's mood. A part your concept never recorded is n/a, not a mismatch; that distinction once carried a 100% false-positive rate.

Names lifted from your text are checked against who said them. A quote attributed to someone the passage doesn't put behind it is marked, never passed through.

A build that claims a seam has to spell it. When the Bench says it clipped clem from clemency, the code rebuilds the word from those exact letters; a seam that doesn't reassemble fails.

What a run cost, and what it retried

What a run cost, in requests rather than renders. Every real HTTP attempt a run makes is recorded once — which component, which stage, which attempt, how long, what came back, and whether another call was in flight beside it. A retried call is three attempts, not one. Attempts that failed are recorded too, because a run that produced nothing is exactly the run whose history you need. What is never recorded: your passage, the prompt, or any credential. And a rate limit is not something Nikodemus tries to outwait in nine seconds — if the provider names a wait that fits the run's disclosed budget it waits exactly that once, and otherwise it stops and hands the retry back to you, because a call that times out may still have cost money.

A broken answer is not retried behind your back. If a reply dies before the model has written anything, Nikodemus tries again inside a budget it has already declared. If it dies after the model started writing, it stops: that request was generated and billed, and quietly asking again would spend twice and could return a different answer with nothing saying so. The retry is yours to make. Nikodemus is also the only thing retrying — the underlying client's own retries are switched off, so the number of attempts on the record is the number of requests that were actually made, and every attempt records which client library made it.

The room — where writing happens

What comes back to the room comes back as notes, not as text. A workup excavates; it does not return. So a reading, an objection, a comparison, a road, an invented example, or the difference an anchor could not carry can be carried back — kept beside the draft under Revision notes while you revise. Carrying means only this may be useful while revising. It is not accepting, supporting, verifying, or approving anything, and it never puts a word into your draft: a contradicted proposal carried for its energy is still contradicted, an invented example still says invented, a recall-only parallel is still recall-only, and those labels ride with the excerpt into the record, onto the tray, and into anything you copy out. The page only names what you carried; the excerpt and its standing are read from the run's own record, so a carry's standing is a fact the record holds, never a claim the page makes, and a name that fits nothing or several things is refused rather than guessed. Opening the notes disturbs nothing in the room — not the text, the caret, the selection, the scroll, or the undo history. A workup examined one text; if the room now holds a different one, the notes say so and wait for your choice rather than attaching themselves to a draft they never read. Which text that was is settled by the run's recorded lineage — a card inside a Go Deep result belongs to a component, a component to the run that read your draft — and each hop is written into the carry. Your draft stays in this browser; the notes live in the record, and ride in the Vault. You write the second draft.

⤢ full page opens the writing room: warm yellow on deep blue, type larger than any input box, and nothing else — no counter, no timer, no praise. Aa changes the face, the size, the measure and how the letters arrive, and remembers your choice on this device. The letters land in one of three ways: Settle, a soft drop with a slight overshoot; Ink, which splatters on the way in and is plain text the moment it lands; and Plain, no motion at all. Nothing continues moving after a letter has arrived, and none of it is part of what you wrote. Tab indents a paragraph's width rather than leaving the writing — a selection across lines indents every line, ⇧Tab takes a level back off, and one undo takes back the whole indent. To leave with the keyboard: Esc then Tab. Spell check is the browser's own, which marks and offers and never rewrites; autocorrect is off, because a phone keyboard is most confident that a coined word is a mistake. Esc leaves.

Guardrails belong on consequential actions, not on language. That erudition-meets-filth register is part of your voice—the Trickster-Sage and the grotesquerie at the same table. Nikodemus is supposed to give that voice more room and more structure, not wash its mouth out with soap.

⫞ split puts the room beside the page itself — the same shelf, cards, documents and controls fitted into half the window, not a miniature lookup and not a recolour. ⇄ swaps sides, the divider drags, either half expands and comes back, and through all of it the draft, the caret, both scroll positions and whatever was open stay exactly where you left them. Your side and your divider are remembered.

Everything you type is written to disk the moment you submit it — before any model is contacted, so a failed run or a closed laptop never costs you your words. And the page does not reset: the run you left is still there, still streaming if it was streaming. Starting fresh is a thing you click, never a thing that happens to you.

Walking somewhere else no longer disturbs the room. The Bench, the Map, a Room, Trails, the Recovery Review and the Investigation Rooms open inside this page rather than replacing it, beside a writing room that is never touched — so a draft keeps its caret, its selection, its undo history, its scroll and the layout you were in, not merely its words. Before, the words came back from this browser's own store and everything else was gone. Each of those places is still its own document under its own rules, and each still answers on its own address if you open it directly. Two take the whole window on purpose: the anatomy (its door is in the header, above), and pairing — a pairing screen drawn inside an unpaired page would be a lie about what is locked.

Inquiry — a question, kept

A question you mean to work gets a place of its own. It is kept exactly as you asked it, however tangled — a narrowed or corrected question becomes a descendant of it, never a replacement — and the branches you open, and what became of each, stay with it. A branch you abandon keeps why and what the failure revealed, because the throw that did not stick is the half worth having. Nothing in an inquiry is a ruling until you promote it. Reopen it in a year and it is standing where you left it.

It reads the question back to you before it does anything else. A tangled question usually means two or three different things at once, and those things need different evidence. So the first thing you get is not an answer — it is two to four ways your question could be read, the assumptions sitting inside it, and the terms that could go either way. Take one, take all of them, reword any of them, or write your own. Taking several makes separate branches, never one blended question. Your wording becomes a descendant of the proposal; the proposal stays where it is.

You can also ask about the question itself — am I assuming resemblance implies borrowing? Those are kept apart from questions about the world, and marked so: nothing can hang an answer off one. A question about your question can never quietly become a finding about the subject.

The reader proposes; it does not answer, look anything up, or know anything about the subject — and it says so on its own face. It cannot state a fact even by accident: a reading is a label, a scope and what evidence it would need, and every word it claims to quote from your question is checked against your question, with anything it invented dropped and shown to you. It costs one model call, and the panel names the lane and the model before you spend it. Searching your record, going outside for sources, and trial are still unbuilt, each named as such with its reason.

The Work Room — a change of scale

Click Enter on a linked source entry and you are inside the work itself — The Fall, not the run that mentioned it. A work is an identity you create: its id is never inferred from a normalized title, so Camus's novel and the fall of Genesis can never collide however their titles match. Everything else is links — owner rulings, made one at a time, append-only, each carrying its origin; relinking supersedes with the lineage kept, and nothing links itself. The room gathers what already exists and writes nothing by itself: Passages (the work's words — only mechanically retrieved imported text, never a recollection), Readings (what your runs said about it, never rendered as its words), Variations (each edition, translation, or excerpt its own document — editions are never blended), Paths (the recorded, reconstructed, and declared roads your record already holds — the room draws no new ones), and Outside Nikodemus: Wikipedia as the lobby, not the courtroom, plus templated doors to scholarly search. Doors only open — nothing is imported by opening them, no click ever changes a record, and what you found becomes a record only when you save it and say so, with an append-only access history and no authority score. Read in split puts the edition beside your writing using the workspace that already exists — no third pane.

The Clinic — where authorities stay separate

/clinic is a room for documents that carry institutional weight — a hospital policy, a professional guideline current or retired, a device label, an independent study, a manufacturer's document — each admitted by you, declared by you: role, issuer, dates (a value, unknown, or not applicable — never a guess), status, facility, scope. Every source speaks in its own institutional voice, never blended: ask the room a question and each admitted source answers with its exact passages under its role and status, side by side; a role with nothing admitted says “No admitted source of this role is present” — never that medicine has no answer. Whether two passages disagree is a judgment, so it exists only as a model proposal with the passages attached or as your ruling — the page never claims it on its own. Supersession and family membership are rulings, never inferred from a title.

The room answers questions about documents and never about a patient. Two gates stand at its door. The question gate refuses a question about a particular patient — yours, this one, the one in bed 4, a person described by age and sex — whether or not an identifier appears, because this is a policy-and-evidence lane, not clinical decision support; it does not advise, choose a treatment, or overrule an order. The document gate looks for identifiers, not the word “patient”: a guideline's ordinary clinical language is admitted; an MRN or account number with a value, a date of birth with an actual date, a name paired with a record field, is refused with no override; a document that merely resembles a case note is held for inspection — in memory only, on no disk, seen by no model, in no Keeper entry and no Vault — until you cancel it or attest that it is a lawful reference document. Nothing about a question is ever kept, accepted or refused; a refusal leaves only the fact of itself: time, lane, rule. An institution's name is never treated as patient information. Restricted material stays on this machine and out of every public artifact.

The Bench — working on a word you already kept

⚒ Bench opens a kept word and hands you its parts: a record if your own run wrote one, or clearly-labelled guesses if it didn't. A model splits your definition into the pieces any rework must carry; you edit those pieces and confirm them before anything runs, because a model can split your meaning wrong, and a wrong piece aims every step after it at the wrong target. Each piece gets a role in plain words — required means remove this and it becomes a different concept; tension is the contradiction that gives it its charge. You declare how the pieces relate — in your own verbs, not a preset menu: X conceals Y, X becomes evidence against Y — because four nouns in a bag are not a concept.

The payoff is the concept, not the coin. Build the concept returns a statement and its anatomy, with an honest accounting of every ingredient; a lost required piece and a silently dropped relation are both reported in code. Every round keeps its own evidence — the untouched raw model output, byte for byte, plus which model built it and the exact definition it built from — and a field too long for the page is cut with a note saying so, the full text surviving in the raw. This is measured, not hoped: in a controlled pair of preserved rounds, declaring relations made the built anatomy reorganize around them, including in fields no relation touched. Naming comes after, only if you ask, and keep the existing name is always the first option, inserted by code: it stands unless the reviewer stakes a real improvement, so the correct answer “no name beats the one it has” is finally one the tool can give. The coinage lab survives behind Try a coined form — meaning first, structure second, language third, coinage last, and sometimes never.

The coinage lab, when you do want a coin

Four diagnoses first — meaning, construction, expectation, sayability — with no overall score, because “awkward” hides which of the four is wrong. Lines with a right answer somewhere are tagged checkable — not checked. Then materials and a method, with no limit on materials: pick everything on the screen if you want; a coin of eleven stems is a bad coin, not an invalid request, and anything a build never touched is named underneath it, read off the letters each build declared. The seam check proves only where the letters came from — a coin can keep the ancestry of its letters and lose the ancestry of its sense. A part you mark 🔒 is a warning, not a gate. Overrule any verdict; it is written down and nothing else happens — the corrections pile up so any future second reviewer can be measured against them rather than trusted.

What accumulates The record is the product. These are the shapes it takes once you have ruled.
The Library — where kept things live

Library is every word this tool has ever put in front of you — in alphabetical order, nothing ever falling off — each under the ruling it carries: kept, reworked, set aside, or undecided, which is most of them and is the point. Click a ruling chip to see everything under it. Alternate names for one concept fold under the concept they name.

Under the tally sits a second band of square chips: what the record says, beside what you ruled. Friction objected; an established term already covers this; the anchor is not in the source text; nothing was checked. They are not a second verdict and they never add up to a score — a word with nothing against it gets no chip at all, because silence is the good state. An entry reading “Friction: reject · you: accepted” is a word the critic argued against and you kept, which is a normal outcome and not a conflict to resolve.

Friction's verdict is about craft — is this a well-made word. The support box below it is about grounding — did the quoted passage establish this. They answer different questions, so a word can survive on craft while its anchor fails to license the claim, and that is not the tool contradicting itself.

No ruling is final. Rule again whenever you like; the log is append-only, so the earlier ruling is kept, the rulings that disagree say so, and the latest one counts. Accepting something that repeats a definition you already own puts the question to you first, and keeping it anyway is now recorded with your reason — a definition can outgrow one word, but a duplicate with no note saying the question was asked is a mislabelled entry in a corpus meant to be read back later.

The meaning is yours to write. Open any word and write the meaning yourself — a run's definition is a proposal. The sentence you replace is kept and labelled, and the grounding resets, because every check ever run on that word was run against the sentence you just retired. A reworked meaning likewise does not inherit its parent's evidence. Run it again on this puts your own definition back through Friction and the lexicon check.

Take it out of here exports the lexicon, your writing, the data, or the whole corpus with a checksum for every file — download, share sheet, or PDF, with the manifest travelling separately, because a receipt inside the box it vouches for vouches for nothing.

The sources

Every rabbithole travels to real works, and every thread writes down what the source shows in the source's own terms, forbidden from mentioning the concept that came looking. Sources turns that around: the works indexed as themselves — read about the Passover Seder without reading about whichever of your words reached it. Who came, when, and what the reviewer ruled folds beneath each entry. Where two runs described one source, both accounts are kept — difference is not disagreement. All of it is recall, never retrieval, and each account names the run that wrote it. And when an entry belongs to a work you created, its row says so — Enter The Fall — the door from recall about a work into the work's own room, where retrieval lives.

The Map — where things connect

🗺 Map is the whole corpus drawn as it happened — every run a box, every recorded connection a road, folded into day columns you can travel. ◫ Aerial shows all of it in one glance: every day, its runs, and the words you accepted, click a day to dive. The Wayfinder takes two concepts and finds every honest route at once, like the alternatives on a road map: Direct, Lineage, Well-trodden, and History — the chain of runs your thinking actually took. Every road identifies itself: recorded, reconstructed from snapshot, declared by you, or inferred. Inferred is a model's proposal, checked in code against what the map actually contains, editable, and a road only when you declare it — a ratified proposal keeps both halves of its history, proposed by the model and ruled real by you. Three percent of shelf pairs are connected at all, so “no road exists” is the usual true answer, delivered with the island sizes instead of a manufactured path. A plotted route can be handed to an analysis run: claims marked “from the record” must cite the route's own roads or they are demoted to interpretation in code, and interpretation stays labelled as a reading, never promoted by citation. Σ Stats counts what you actually did — searches, roads declared, proposals ratified, the archipelago itself — and every Wayfinder act lands in a behavioral log kept as raw counts, never conclusions: evidence of how you travel your corpus, deliberately left uninterpreted.

What holds it Two organs that keep and never produce: one restores a verified past exactly, one narrates and never decides.
The Vault — restoration, not regeneration

The corpus is sealed into an encrypted vault on every server start and after changes settle, to a destination you chose, under a recovery secret shown once and proven twice — password manager and paper — because losing it makes every vault permanently unreadable, and that is accepted on purpose. A vault is real only when a copy fetched back from off this machine has been opened and verified; the record keeps which vaults were drilled and never prunes one it has no sealed row for. The Vault restores a verified past; it regrows and improves nothing. One process is the corpus's only writer at a time, and the page's vault strip turns red the moment sealing stalls.

The Keeper — custody of the narration, not authority over the record

The Book has a resident narrator, summoned and never scheduled: ask it to close a stretch of work and it writes an entry in a voice you and it author together. Its speech comes in three classes and each is marked — event claims that must resolve to records (an invalid one is shown as UNGROUNDED, never quietly re-classed), inferences that are revisable proposals about you and never facts about you, and flourish. It may have opinions; it may disagree with you in its voice forever; it may be wrong, and its wrongness stays inspectable — every entry is kept, revised, or rejected by you, and the full model input for every entry is preserved before the call. It may interpret you; it may form toward you, but it must never form you silently. Off stops its adaptation as well as its speech.

What it will not claim The limits are part of the constitution, not an appendix to it.
What it does not do

It never checks whether it actually looked. Every check asks “is this claim supported?”; none asks “did I search before saying nothing exists?” An absence claim is the least trustworthy thing this tool produces.

Sprout threads and Refract terms are generated from recall; only the review searches. Sprout has never once come back empty — fifty-eight runs, two hundred ninety-five threads, not one saying “no parallel exists.” A stage that cannot say no is producing parallels rather than finding them; the reviewer then walks most back, with about two in three arriving strained. And a third of the sprout reviews never searched — thirty-eight of fifty-eight consulted live sources; the rest are recall reviewed by recall, and each run lists which it was. (The older measurement here read “Half the sprout reviews never searched”; the share has improved and the sentence stays honest by saying so.)

Taking a word apart is not looking it up. Etymologies and first-attestation claims are a model's account of the record, reviewed by a pass that may or may not have searched — the run says which. The famous frauds of this genre are all neat stories, so a plausible one is exactly what to distrust.

Duplicate detection compares definitions as text; two wordings of one idea pass as separate concepts. It offers, never gates — which is why the decline is written down.

The archetype stage checks shape and nothing else. It is the one stage with nothing outside itself to test against, which is exactly why it is the one to distrust.

Two in five lineage links on the shelf are reconstructed rather than recorded — it was half when first measured, and the share improves because sprouts store their parent while refractions and revisions never did: theirs is derived and every derived link says so on its face. The Map draws recorded lineage and the roads you have declared — it never draws an inferred road into the permanent geography; proposals appear only inside your plotted route and vanish unless you make them real.

Six of the oldest kept words once had no meaning stored anywhere — accepted before definitions were written at judgment time. All six have since been given meanings by hand through the shelf; the history of the hole is kept on their entries. Twenty runs from 22–23 August still have a receipt and no snapshot — titles survive, reasoning doesn't (re-verified today: exactly twenty, sixteen and four). Nothing has been lost either way since.

An anchor is one quotation, and some ideas live in a sequence — the model picks one span and the support check rightly reports it doesn't carry the whole thing. When that happens the evidence selection failed, not necessarily the idea. Compound anchors don't exist yet — and the Documents wing now measures the demand for them: “insufficient span” rulings whose suggested reselection still wouldn't suffice are the signal, countable from the log.

The Bench has no dictionary, word list or corpus wired in. Its etymologies and syllable claims are tagged checkable — not checked, in code — and the tag is all you get; a true etymology and a folk one look identical there.

Documents reads five formats — EPUB, saved web pages, plain text, PDF, and DOCX — each under its own named, versioned extractor, never a shared “universal” one. A scanned PDF with no text layer is refused plainly rather than mangled: there is no OCR. Its search matches exact text, not meanings. An ingredient crossed from a document is stored with its span, but the Bench does not yet offer it when you open a word — the two still meet by hand.

The Clinic is one room, not a medical library. Room One holds six seats and no bulk ingestion; its answers are exact retrieved passages, never a synthesis, and it is never clinical decision support. The screens at its door are heuristic backstops and say so — the lane's shape (questions about documents, not patients) is the real control. The room is shipped but awaiting shadow-mode validation on real nights; until then its usefulness is a claim, not a finding.

The works registry links nothing by itself. Creating a work titled exactly like a source entry links zero entries — every link is a ruling you make, one at a time, and unlinked entries render exactly as they always did. Declaring a Wikipedia page or a Wikidata QID fetches nothing; the outside shelf's doors open searches and record nothing; an access status exists only where you stated one. The Map's own page has no link-from-map control yet — links are made from Sources rows and inside the room. And two works named The Fall stay two works forever: the tool will never merge them, which also means a duplicate you created by mistake is yours to retract, never its to clean up.

Media transcribes nothing. Nikodemus has no speech recognition: you supply the SRT or WebVTT, and a plain-text transcript is refused because it cannot anchor time. No platform is touched — no YouTube, no RSS, no stream capture; a recording enters only as a file you own, and duration is the player's live reading, never a stored claim. The parser's rules are pinned by revision: cue lines join with single spaces, WebVTT voice tags become speakers, other markup is stripped and counted, malformed cues are skipped and counted — never silently. Speakers exist only where the file declares them. And the model's support question does not run on media claims yet — only your own ruling does.

Nothing here reads your writing. The writing room is a room; the tool sees only what you hand it by pressing a button. The whole thing runs as a local server on your own machine with no account or telemetry of its own. Text leaves the machine only through a lane you explicitly invoke: bounded model payloads go to your configured model provider through your own API key, and the live-review lanes may send search queries through their configured search service. Nikodemus records what it supplied and what was consulted; third-party handling remains governed by those providers and your account settings — that part is not Nikodemus’s to promise. Its canonical records are plain JSON files on your disk (and JSONL logs), readable without this tool and exportable in full with checksums; imported originals stay byte-intact in their native format, and the search index is SQLite — a rebuildable index, never the authority. No account, no telemetry, no copy anywhere you can't see.

A partial workup is not a reading. A deep run splits your passage into components and forges each one separately. When some of them fail — a dead model call, a rate limit, a provider that never answered — the page says so first, before anything else on it: PARTIAL WORKUP — n of m components completed. No overall reading exists. That line is a fact, not an explanation, so it is never folded behind a mark. Coverage is not completion: the extractor may account for every part of your passage and still have analysed one component of six, and the page states both numbers rather than letting the first speak for the second. Every count is scoped to what actually completed. A failure to run is not a finding — nothing judged the components that did not complete, in either direction — and each of them keeps its own retry, which disturbs nothing that finished.

Whether any of this beats a plain prompt

Every check inside the tool asks is this claim supported; none asks is this pipeline better than one line of prompt. scripts/blind.py asks it: real words from your corpus run through the constrained stage and through a bare prompt, every tell stripped, shuffled, and you pick — then it tells you how often a coin flip does that well. Under six decided pairs it refuses to conclude anything.

Nikodemus is the concept lens of a larger family — the same receipts-not-verdicts machinery that elsewhere investigates public records, corporate conduct, and news coverage, here pointed inward at ideas. Tap anything under Recent to reopen it. “Another round” retries briefed on what failed. Runs keep working when you close the tab, and whatever was going is waiting under Where you left off.
