Legend & what this map does NOT claim
◆ concept   ● word-form   ★ external work   ⬠ translation   ▣ source text   ◇ component   ⊘ contradicts its own anchor
Two judgments per item, never one. Friction: reject is the critic's advice and decides nothing. you: accepted is your ruling and is the only thing that settles anything. An item can carry both — Friction arguing against a word you kept is a normal, recorded disagreement, not an error.
solid = recorded lineage · dashed arc = same title recurring · thick solid arc = different titles, one shared concept (carried by Revise/Wordify) · broken + ⚠ = disputed verdicts · faint = rejected, kept as ghost · green ring = you accepted it
Wayfinder roads identify themselves: recorded written to the log as it happened · reconstructed from snapshot the event happened, the road was written down late · declared you ruled it exists · inferred a model proposed it just now — not part of the record, editable, and real only if you declare it. Lineage, Direct and History never infer; “No road exists” is a result, not an error.
