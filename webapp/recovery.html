What this is

Six acceptances survived the identity migration as receipt-only: the record says you accepted them, and holds the run's receipt, and nothing else — no result snapshot, so no definition, and no shelf entry. Every ruling is a new judgment event citing the old acceptance and its receipt, with its own clock and epoch. The queue is never rewritten; when all six are ruled, this page and Home's band empty through the record.
