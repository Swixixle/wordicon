▶Documents

Whole works, kept as themselves: a book, a chapter, a saved news article. Bringing one in costs zero model calls — the file is stored byte-intact, split deterministically into sections, paragraphs and sentences, and indexed for exact search. Re-ingest the identical file under the same reader revisions and you get identical anchors; that is a property the tests hold, not a hope — and a revised reader makes a new immutable reading, never a rewrite. Anything the reader had to skip or could not parse is written on the document's face, never silently smoothed over.

▶Media

Recordings you own — a lecture, a podcast episode you have the file for, an interview — kept byte-intact with zero model calls, beside the transcripts you supply. The transcript is a derivative that can be wrong; the recording remains the source, and every transcript version — publisher's, platform's, your own correction — is kept separately, never overwritten and never merged. Nothing here transcribes, fetches, or touches a platform: SRT or WebVTT come from you, because a transcript without timestamps cannot anchor time.

▶Sources

Every work, figure and episode a sprout has ever travelled to — read as itself, not as a parallel to one of your words. Which of your concepts arrived here is folded under each entry, not above it.

All of this is recall, not retrieval. The locator says where to check; it is not a record that anything was checked. Where more than one run described a source, every account is kept — merging them would invent an authority nothing here has, and difference is not disagreement. Even a clean account chose its emphasis: it was written by a run hunting a resemblance, and the wording check below catches the concept's name and copied phrasing, nothing subtler. This whole index is rebuilt from the runs on every load — delete a run and its accounts leave with it; a source with no account gains one the next time a run travels there, dated then, never backfilled.

What is Nikodemus? — and proof

See the anatomy — how the parts cooperate, challenge one another, stay constrained, and preserve history; dormant until you set it in motion.

Nikodemus (formerly Wordicon) is a private environment for developing concepts beside your sources and your writing. It keeps your concepts, sources, and judgments separate, connected, and reviewable: nothing you ruled on is overwritten, nothing the model proposed is treated as your conclusion, and material it was forbidden to accept is refused before it is stored. It began as a word-coining tool; the forge is still inside.

What it refuses to pretend

Each line below is a verbatim excerpt of the clause it links to — not a summary of it. A summary written here in its own words would be a second constitution that drifts quietly from the first; the suite fails if any of these stops matching the law word for word.

  • It never checks whether it actually looked.
  • A failure to run is not a finding
  • A passing check may be quiet; a failing check may not.
  • The Vault restores a verified past; it regrows and improves nothing
  • Under six decided pairs it refuses to conclude anything.
  • the critic advises and never decides
  • The readers answer separately and stay separate: no conference, no verdict, no score, and nothing here combines them.
  • Saved is an acknowledgement of the latest words, never a timer

Read the full constitution — all five movements, whole, with a table of contents. That page is the law; this panel is an orientation and is bound by it.

When on, the record keeps ids and event types only — which entry was surfaced, opened, selected, reused, cited, sprouted or refracted from, revised, linked, exported — never any text.
