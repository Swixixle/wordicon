## Slice H — the review of 6e5b59c, corrected

The reviewer's three findings and the completeness items, each fixed and pinned:

- **Finding 1 — a recovery could lose newer writing.** When a save committed on the server but its reply was lost, reopening compared the envelope's base revision with the head, found them different, and discarded every word typed after that save (and overwrote their recovery copy). Now `session.js` reconciles the divergence through the exact saved request: the pending request is resent under its own id and payload; the server answers `repeated` with the revision it made; if that revision is the head the envelope is rebased onto it and the later words are recovered and saved as the next revision — no duplicate, nothing combined; if another writer has advanced the head past it, both versions are kept as a named conflict (`unreconciled`) and nothing is merged by guesswork. `editor.js` §20 (same-tab reload after a committed-but-lost reply, body edit), §21 (a fresh tab, a formatting-only edit; the other tab's envelope marked taken), §22 (the head moved on: keep both, both on the server under their own ids) — in Chromium and WebKit; the browser-side sabotage pass adds "discard an envelope based on an older revision than the head" and "never resend the pending request", both caught by name. The reviewer's reproduction (`repro.cjs`) now prints `LATEST WORDS LOST false`.
