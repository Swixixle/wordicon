Open Case integrity checkpoint — 2026-10-18

The shared workspace keeps source collection, answer validity and workflow
lifecycle separate. Completed means required acquisition is ready AND a valid
answer reports the question answered. Adequacy is explicitly the model's own
assessment; citations are checked structurally, not a proof of interpretation.

A judicial-biography proposal declares FJC required, CourtListener optional,
and finance/regulatory sources inapplicable. Unimplemented sources are named
separately. The contract snapshot is local; availability is checked at Start.
The producer records actual capabilities and attempts in its result.

New source semantics are checked against the pinned signed package, including
source text and classification. Old originals never change. Explicit signed
corrections bind to original result, deposition payload and individual finding
hashes; verified corrections appear as derived views. A follow-up freezes the
correction set along with the source artifact.

Source lineage records captures, authoritative record IDs, substantive revisions
and provenance families independently. URLs and renderer hashes alone cannot
establish independence. The answer guard detects unsupported positive claims
of corroboration; this heuristic is not a complete semantic truth checker.
Deflate reimports/new interpretations share their original reading identity;
transcript-content duplicate groups are separate from source identity.

One model attempt defaults to 4,096 output tokens. Explicit answer recovery uses
up to 8,192 and only the frozen saved evidence, with no producer collection.
A held original, raw partial response, provider usage and errors remain kept.
No hidden retry or resending an ambiguous Start. Known recovery operations are
reconciled after restart even without a producer job ID.

Before a billable dispatch, an append-only reservation checks a conservative
request estimate against the disclosed $0.50 allowance. The current supported
rate card is Anthropic Sonnet 5 standard direct global API, dated2026-10-18.
Settlements preserve input/output/cache counts, response identity and receipt
hash. Estimates are distinct from provider-billed totals. Unknown usage/rates
remain unknown; duplicates of the same response are not charged twice.
Settings, operation and ancestry totals use the same existing event ledger.
Legacy reconciliation appends linked accounting corrections. Other older
operations lacking accounting remain explicitly unaccounted, not zero.

Offline gates: tests/test_evidence_lineage.py, test_operation_costs.py,
test_opencase_integrity_workflow.py (includes inherited synthesis/workflow
regressions), test_global_constraints.py, tests/journeys/opencase.js.
Live acceptance is recorded separately under integration/verification; passing
fixtures alone is not evidence of a useful live investigation.
